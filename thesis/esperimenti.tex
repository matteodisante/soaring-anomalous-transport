\chapter*{Scope}
This volume collects the experimental and modelling chapters following Chapter~2
of the thesis. The introduction, dataset description, and Appendices 2.A and 2.B
are in the companion thesis volume, \emph{Anomalous Transport in Soaring Flights}
(\texttt{main.pdf}). Cross-references to that material point to the thesis PDF.
The original chapter numbering, 3--7, is retained.

\paragraph{Material incorporated into the thesis.}
Passages printed in \textcolor{red!70!black}{red} have been incorporated, with revised
estimators and newly measured results, into Chapter~\ref{ch:fixed-transport} of the
thesis. Their original wording and numerical results are retained here as a record
of the exploratory analysis; the current definitions and estimates are in the thesis.
Unmarked material, including the anisotropy and PCA analyses, has not been transferred.
