\chapter{Overview}

This part collects the implementation- and computation-level detail behind the choices
made in the main text: the exact code module and script that produced each figure or
table, the configuration values in force, sample sizes and computation times, and the
status of each piece (implemented and tested, designed but not yet built, or planned). It
is organised as a document within the document: its own chapters\flow{, numbered
independently of the appendices,} mirror the body chapter by chapter. It is not meant to be read start to end -- the body links to the relevant
place at the point where a detail was trimmed out of the text, via a cross-reference such
as ``(implementation details: Sec.~3.5.2)''.

\rev{--- (all of the above, in order)} & \rev{\path{../regenerate.sh}} &

\rev{--- (a check, not a product)} & \rev{\protect\path{../check_reproducible.py}} &

  in the raw archive, run through \path{run_flight} as it stands, and compared with the
  stored rows column by column---row count, every boolean flag exactly, every kinematic

  for reasons no invariant check can see: a run interrupted and resumed from a different
  commit, a rule edited while the archive was being written, a version bumped without
  re-running. On the present dataset \num{500} flights over both disciplines reproduce
  identically, the largest departure being \SI{0.016}{\metre}---the \texttt{float32}
  quantum at a coordinate of \SI{100}{\kilo\metre}.} \\
