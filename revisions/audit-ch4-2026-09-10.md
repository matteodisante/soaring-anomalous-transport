# Chapter 4 audit — 2026-09-10

The numerical examples below belong to the initial audit of the saved model. The
complete rebuild refits and applies segmentation after cleaning 2.2.0; its final
results and interpretation must be checked before replacing the historical values
with current conclusions. Progress is tracked in `request-checklist-2026-09-11.md`.

The audit checks the HMM mathematics against the implementation and the saved models,
the meaning of its diagnostics, the annotation workflow, and every figure currently
included in Chapter 4. It does not establish human-label
accuracy or regenerate the complete archive.

## Corrections made

- **Reconstruction influence was incompletely masked.** A 30-s feature window could
  include a vertical SG derivative influenced by reconstructed altitude outside that
  feature window. Preprocessing now propagates `z_derivative_reconstructed` across the
  complete vertical fit support (implemented by the Chapter 2 audit). Fresh segmentation
  requires that flag and includes it in its window support. Missing legacy input is
  rejected rather than silently treated as fully safe. Saved-feature redecoding remains
  available, with its older support explicitly identified.
- **The mathematical feature definition now matches the order of operations.** The
  code linearly interpolates native `abs(omega)` before integrating, which differs at a
  reversal from taking `abs` after interpolating signed `omega`. The chapter gives this
  distinction and the triangle-inequality proof of `0 <= C_omega <= 1`. Numerical
  roundoff is clipped to those analytic bounds.
- **The library stopping flag was not proof of convergence.** In the saved selected
  fits, the final log-likelihood gains were **−0.12375173997** (paragliders, iteration
  72/200) and **−1.34848345513** (hang gliders, 24/200), while the library's flag was true.
  Future fits record convergence only for a finite final gain in `[0, tol)`. Non-finite
  final restart scores are rejected. The meaning of old flags is preserved explicitly
  when older artifacts are loaded or saved.
- **The covariance floor was being misunderstood.** In hmmlearn 0.3.3, `min_covar=0.001`
  regularizes initialization, not every covariance M step. The actual default full
  covariance update adds the scalar `covars_prior=0.01` to *every entry* of the weighted
  scatter matrix before dividing by occupancy; this is a rank-one addition, not an
  eigenvalue floor. Minimum fitted eigenvalues were about `5.45e-8` and `2.95e-8`.
  Chapter 4 states the actual update and its limitation.
- The exact provisional naming score is stated: highest vertical mean is climb;
  transition maximizes standardized `mean_v_h - mean_abs_turn_rate - 0.5*coherence`
  among the remaining components. Gaussian marginalization, the transition policy,
  Viterbi and posterior use agree with the code.
- Absorbing-row handling and zero-denominator classification metrics are explicit.
  The future efficiency bound `R/L <= 1` requires a consistent horizontal curve and
  path length; it cannot be assumed exactly for an independently smoothed derivative.
- **Report assumptions survive a fresh rebuild.** The audit first reproduces archived
  labels with their saved decoder, then reconstructs a 4D/no-prior reference from the
  same fitted parameters. It no longer assumes the canonical archive must be old 4D
  labels. Coverage, coherence widths, ablation counts and fit/support status in the
  chapter are generated macros, preventing those numerical sentences from surviving
  a new cleaning unchanged.
- The older report's illustrative trajectory selection now uses validation flights,
  avoiding additional test-flight inspection during routine regeneration.

## Figures and prose

All seven currently used numerical Chapter 4 figures were regenerated from
saved features and the current decoder. The common semantic palette is blue/orange/green,
with letters on longer phase runs and distinct posterior line styles. Phase strips use
solid runs rather than separate rectangles that produce distracting hairlines. Model
figures use compact T/S/C legends, one-based restart numbers, and likelihood differences
relative to the best fit rather than unreadable additive tick offsets. Coverage uses a
separate categorical palette. Text placement was inspected in rendered PDFs; remaining
model-label clipping was corrected with a tight bounding box.

The chapter explains the measurement and assumptions before interpreting
outputs. Repeated declarations of what the chapter does not prove were reduced. They
still retain the qualifications that materially limit the conclusions: overlapping
windows, provisional state names, imposed geometric latent dwell times, censoring,
and the lack of independent human labels.

## Annotation pack and user action

The original pack has 40 flights and no human intervals. Its existing candidate rows
were compared exactly with the source phase rows before `pack_provenance.json` was
written. Checksums bind its local windows and kinematics. When mounted, changed cleaned
fixes or split assignments make the GUI refuse a stale pack. Model calibration may
change predictions and naming without invalidating the blinded inputs. A copied intact
pack can be used offline; source freshness cannot then be checked.

After cleaning, create a new versioned pack; preserve the old directory and all labels.
The combined rebuild command does this. Start with `--split train`, then validation,
and reserve test predictions/scores until choices are fixed. The old pack contains
about 10.1 hours of training trajectory and 14.2 hours of held-out trajectory; these
are trajectory durations, not estimates of annotation labour.

Known inspected nominal-test flights remain excluded. The flight split does not ensure
independence across repeated pilots, days or regions. Candidate selection covers varied
conditions and is not a prevalence-weighted probability sample.

The calibration code currently uses Hungarian agreement on the pre-calibration raw
component path. With a semantic prior, re-decoding after renaming changes that path;
it does not guarantee the globally best final train agreement over all permutations.
The chapter and guide state this limitation. Human calibration and post-calibration
validation remain necessary; no semantic accuracy is claimed.

## Canonical regeneration order

For each discipline: `segment_flights.py train`, `apply`, `coverage`, all with the
current configuration. Then `generate_segmentation_report.py`,
`generate_decoder_audit.py`, and `prepare_annotation_pack.py --output-dir <new-pack>`.
A newly trained canonical model already stores current decoding choices; no duplicate
`--current-decoder` export is needed. The latter remains supported for updating an
older fit in a separate directory.

The report, audit and annotation commands have been checked against
`configs/rebuild.yaml`. A subsequent read-only orchestration review found that the
full source hash initially omitted authored thesis/BibTeX files, and subprocess
cancellation initially terminated only the direct child. Both findings were sent to
the parent implementation owner. No full rebuild was run during this audit.

## Verification and sources

The focused segmentation/labeler/report suite passed **57 tests**, with 24 upstream
hmmlearn/NumPy deprecation warnings. The additional annotation/calibration freshness
regression and audit tests passed **8 tests** after the pack checks were refined.
Changed segmentation code and scripts pass Ruff. Numerical figure regeneration
reproduces the archived example paths and reports the unchanged current-decoder
comparison counts (612/2420 paraglider and 255/910 hang-glider decisions).

Primary sources checked:

- [hmmlearn 0.3.3 API](https://hmmlearn.readthedocs.io/en/0.3.3/api.html), together with
  the installed `GaussianHMM._do_mstep`, `_init` and `ConvergenceMonitor.converged`
  source. A software bibliography entry was requested from the parent owner.
- [Vilpellet et al., arXiv:2601.01293v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.01293v1): the
  three behavioural names and their ambiguity agree; its original feature/emission
  construction differs from the continuous Gaussian implementation here, as stated.
- [Rabiner's author publication list](https://web.ece.ucsb.edu/Faculty/Rabiner/ece259/publications.html)
  and [university-hosted tutorial PDF](https://www.fceia.unr.edu.ar/prodivoz/Rabiner_1989.pdf):
  forward–backward, likelihood factorization and Viterbi reference.
- [Langrock et al. 2012](https://esajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1890/11-2241.1):
  geometric HMM dwell times and explicit-duration alternatives, with bibliography
  volume 93, issue 11, pages 2336–2342 confirmed.
- [McClintock et al. 2020 author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.10497) and
  [NOAA manuscript](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/27454/noaa_27454_DS1.pdf):
  ecological HMM assumptions and interpretation. Other original algorithm citations
  were handed to the independent bibliography audit for DOI/PDF verification.
