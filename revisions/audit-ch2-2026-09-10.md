# Chapter 2 correctness audit — 10 September 2026

This audit changed methods prose, mathematical deductions, diagnostic preparation,
plotting and verification. It did not regenerate the full cleaned flight archive.
The user-selected 10 m/s vertical quality threshold is unchanged.

## Findings corrected

- **GNSS datum:** a GNSS column is not necessarily ellipsoidal height. IGC-approved
  and published CIVL recorder documents distinguish ellipsoid and geoid conventions.
  The CIVL PDF contains draft annotations, so it is evidence of differing conventions,
  not evidence that every historical HPG logger complied. The parser currently does
  not harmonize this datum; absolute heights and ECEF height inputs retain this limit.
- **ENU geometry:** the full ECEF chord preserves 3D distances under rotation.
  Horizontal projection at the origin gives `R sin(d/R)`, with arc deficit
  `d^3/(6 R^2)`, not the chord deficit `d^3/(24 R^2)`. The leading horizontal
  deficit is 0.51 m at 50 km and 33 m at 200 km. Spherical-distance relative-error
  signs at 45° were also reversed in the previous footnote and are corrected.
- **Savitzky–Golay:** a five-sample window spans four time intervals. Cubic order is
  a modelling choice, not the minimum necessary to estimate velocity. The coefficient
  table uses forward-offset (`use='dot'`) weights; convolution reverses them.
  Terminal `mode='interp'` evaluation is inside the fitted interval, not extrapolation.
- **Noise contribution to MSD:** the previous unfiltered `2σ²` expression was applied
  after smoothing. The text now derives the filtered error covariance and lag-dependent
  MSD contribution. For the five-sample cubic value kernel the variance is `17σ²/35`
  and the one-coordinate non-overlap MSD contribution is `34σ²/35`. The two-coordinate
  MSD adds both contributions. Independence is explicitly an assumption, not a GNSS fact.
- **Filter validation:** removed claims that the residual must be flat, that a Hurst
  exponent guarantees locally cubic paths, that the first MSD decade is entirely
  instrumental, and that archive validation had already certified the window. The exact
  transfer function and residual response now state what follows mathematically.
- **Altitude reconstruction support:** pipeline **2.2.0** adds boolean
  `z_derivative_reconstructed`. It marks all outputs whose vertical polynomial fit uses
  reconstructed input altitude, including terminal fit windows and without crossing a
  segment boundary. `z_reconstructed` retains the original resampling mask. Chapter 4
  consumes the propagated flag; earlier stored tables do not contain it.
- **PSD sampling:** the smoothing diagnostic previously plotted GNSS only for flights
  without a barometer while presenting a three-channel comparison. It now admits each
  channel independently and reports the actual population. The paired channel figure
  uses the same longest uninterrupted valid interval for both channels. Missing or zero
  altitude, invalid GNSS, non-increasing time and gaps longer than 1.5 sampling periods
  split diagnostic support. Cached PSD policy version 2 rejects older prepared samples.
- **PSD attribution:** similar spectra cannot establish quantization as the sole cause
  of a knee or exclude multipath at high frequency. A rounding-noise formula now carries
  both its uniform-error and temporal-independence assumptions. The reported GNSS tail
  fraction compares each GNSS statistic with the *population median* barometric statistic.
- **Memory:** paired PSD preparation no longer retains 6000 full tracks in memory.
  Fix-level histograms stream the long-form Parquet cache; their finite denominators
  include out-of-view values. A cold diagnostic cache is written in bounded batches to a
  temporary file. `generate_preproc_figure.py --rescan` refreshes both raw diagnostic caches.
- **Witness and trimming:** removed obsolete barometric-adoption/GNSS-fallback text,
  unsupported universal circle guarantees, claims that a missed freeze has negligible
  aggregate effect, and a purported trimming-error bound of one persistence time.
  The percentile span has a Gaussian reference value, not an exact sample-invariant width.
- **Interpretation:** finite records are not necessarily correct; equal total seasonal
  retention does not rule out conditional selection bias; first-fix altitude is not a
  terrain DEM; an offset regression does not identify atmospheric temperature, and
  same-site/same-day groups do not hold all atmospheric conditions fixed.
- **Verifier:** finite checks reject infinity as well as NaN. Retained segment counts
  are compared in both directions, and duplicate metadata keys are rejected. Processing
  exceptions (`pipeline_raised`/error stage) cannot masquerade as successful exclusions.
  Gross filtered speed excesses remain operational anomaly gates, not proof of sensor
  corruption. Failed verification does not replace thesis macros.

## Validation and remaining limits

The preprocessing and altitude-diagnostic suite passed **284 tests** after the support
flag and diagnostic-selection corrections (`/tmp/ch2-audit-tests.log`). Added verifier
fixtures cover infinity, a declared segment absent from fixes, duplicate metadata and
processing exceptions. A small streamed-cache fixture checks full-sample denominators.
The final reporting and altitude-diagnostic run completed with **32 tests passed**
(`/tmp/ch2-final-report-tests.log`). These results precede the later documentation-only
edits and do not cover subsequent code changes; later checks are recorded in the consolidated audit.

The revised three-channel SG spectrum and cleaning illustrations were regenerated
and visually checked. The SG-response correction was completed in the consolidated revision.
The paired-altitude PSD, streamed fix-level report and isotropy-panel regenerations
were interrupted and terminated after the user reported excessive machine load.
They are deferred to the next complete cleaning and reporting run; there are no
suspended regeneration jobs to resume.

The final documentation pass corrected the raw reach allowance (50 m), the distinct
recorded-duration floor and elapsed-span ceiling, the 60 s suspect-reporting floor,
and unperformed sensitivity studies. It removed claims that retention plateaus prove
error removal or that small altitude excursions exclude real soaring. The corresponding
trimming and flight-filter docstrings were aligned without algorithm changes. Appendix
table widths and long code references were adjusted; final typesetting is checked by
the consolidated build.

This initial audit used the older cleaning snapshot. Cleaning 2.2.0 has since completed
for both archives; downstream regeneration and final interpretation remain open. No
automatic test establishes unknown real-data error rates; manual labels,
threshold/window sensitivity and a recorder-datum census remain empirical work.
Full-thesis compilation and consolidated validation belong to the complete rebuild.
